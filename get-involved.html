Contact Mark

Share your ideas. Ask a question. Get involved.

Mark wants to hear from residents across Strathroy-Caradoc. Contact the campaign to share a local issue, ask a question, volunteer, request a lawn sign, invite Mark to an event, or stay informed.

Good local government starts with listening. No councillor can know every issue affecting every neighbourhood, rural road, farm, business, or family without hearing directly from residents. Tell Mark what is working, what concerns you, and what needs attention. He will listen, ask informed questions, do the work to understand the issue, and work toward practical solutions.
