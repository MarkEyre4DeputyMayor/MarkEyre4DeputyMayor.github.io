About Mark Eyre

Prepared. Independent. Accountable.

Mark Eyre is seeking the office of Deputy Mayor to bring professional experience, careful judgment, and a strong commitment to public service to Strathroy-Caradoc.

Professional Engineer P.Eng.
Business Training MBA
Farm Owner Walkers Drive

Mark Eyre is a retired professional engineer (P.Eng.) with an MBA. At 64, Mark is bringing his professional experience, independent judgment, and available time to public service.

His background combines engineering analysis, business training, practical problem-solving, and a commitment to careful financial decision-making.

Mark is a farm owner on Walkers Drive in Strathroy-Caradoc and has a direct connection to the municipality’s agricultural, rural, and land-use issues.

He has been actively involved in municipal planning and governance matters, including reviewing planning policy, official-plan and zoning processes, council procedure, and public participation rules.

Mark has appeared before Council as a resident delegate and has worked to ensure residents receive a fair opportunity to be heard.
