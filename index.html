Priorities

Focused on the issues that matter.

Mark’s platform is grounded in transparent government, sound planning, protection of rural and agricultural interests, taxpayer value, and infrastructure that keeps pace with growth.

Transparent Government

Open decisions, meaningful public participation, and clear reasons from Council.

Responsible Planning

Evidence-based land-use decisions that respect rules, services, and the long-term public interest.

Rural Protection

Respect for agricultural land, farming operations, rural communities, and sound planning principles.

Taxpayer Value

Careful spending, strong procurement practices, and attention to long-term costs and risk.

Infrastructure First

Practical planning for water, wastewater, roads, drainage, and municipal capacity before growth outpaces services.
